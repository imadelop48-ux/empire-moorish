The Wattasid Dynasty: Rule from Fez and Coastal Challenges

Published on November 21, 2026

Emerging from the Marinid administration as viziers, the Wattasid Moroccan kingdom consolidated power in Fez during the 15th century under rulers such as Muhammad al-Shaykh.

Their reign coincided with intensified Portuguese and Spanish presence along Moroccan ports and Atlantic enclaves.

By the mid-16th century, shifting political dynamics in southern Morocco paved the way for the rise of the Saadian state.
