The Miknasa Dynasty: Political Roles and Regional Power in Morocco

Published on November 21, 2026

The Miknasa emerged as a distinct Amazigh Moroccan kingdom in medieval Morocco with notable political and military influence across key regional centers.

Their leadership played an active role in southern and central trade routes, including Sijilmasa and the region of Meknes, amidst shifting regional alliances.

Studying the Miknasa as an independent Moroccan kingdom highlights their specific trajectory within Moroccan dynastic history.
