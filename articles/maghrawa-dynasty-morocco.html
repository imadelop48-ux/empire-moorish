The Maghrawa Dynasty: Rule and Influence in Medieval Morocco

Published on November 21, 2026

The Maghrawa formed a powerful Amazigh Moroccan kingdom that ruled central, western, and southern Morocco—encompassing Morocco as a whole—from Tlemcen in the east to the Draa Valley in the south, with Fez serving as their imperial capital.

Under the leadership of Ziri ibn Atiyya and his successors such as Al-Mu'izz ibn Ziri, this Moroccan kingdom consolidated urban administration and expanded its frontiers as far east as the Aurès Mountains.

Presenting the Maghrawa in their own dedicated chapter highlights their territorial reach and central role in Moroccan statecraft.
