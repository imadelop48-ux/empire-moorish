The Almohad Caliphate: Monumental Towers and Imperial Scholarship

Published on November 21, 2026

Emerging in the High Atlas Mountains during the 12th century, the Almohad Moroccan empire was founded by Ibn Tumart and expanded into a vast caliphate under his successor Abd al-Mu'min ibn Ali.

During the reign of Caliph Yaqub al-Mansur—famed for the victory at Alarcos in 1195 CE—this Moroccan empire's architecture reached iconic heights with the Koutoubia in Marrakech, the Hassan Tower in Rabat, and the Giralda in Seville.

The Almohad imperial court also fostered major philosophical and scientific scholarship across the western Islamic world.
