The Saadian Dynasty: Sovereignty, Wadi al-Makhazin, and El Badi Palace

Published on November 21, 2026

Emerging in southern Morocco in the 16th century, the Saadian Moroccan empire unified the country and defended its sovereignty against foreign expansion.

Following the historic victory at the Battle of Wadi al-Makhazin in 1578 CE, Sultan Ahmad al-Mansur al-Dhahabi expanded diplomatic ties with Europe and the Ottoman Empire alongside trans-Saharan trade.

In Marrakech, this Moroccan empire left enduring architectural masterpieces such as El Badi Palace and the ornate Saadian Tombs.
