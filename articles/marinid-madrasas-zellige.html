The Marinid Dynasty: Madrasas, Zellige, and Fez el-Jdid

Published on November 21, 2026

Rising to power in the 13th century, the Marinid Moroccan empire made Fez its imperial capital and built the royal city of Fez el-Jdid.

Sultans such as Abu Yusuf Yaqub, Abu al-Hasan, and Abu Inan Faris patronized historic madrasas including Bou Inania and Al-Attarine.

These institutions showcase the pinnacle of Moroccan decorative arts: geometric zellige tilework, intricate carved stucco, and Atlas cedarwood.
